## Текст статьи (содержательная часть)

The media and entertainment industry is in constant flux with new platforms,
technologies, and consumer behaviors emerging all the time. Staying ahead and
taking advantage of everything requires a deep understanding of your audience
and a data-driven approach to product development.

That's where the 2025 State of Media & Entertainment Product Analytics report
comes in.

### The 2025 State of Media & Entertainment Product Analytics: Data, insights, and more

We crunched the numbers and analyzed 3.72 trillion events across 14.16 billion
unique users from 2,713 media and entertainment companies. Introducing the
2025 State of Media & Entertainment Product Analytics report, a data-driven
deep dive into the most critical metrics across the industry.

The newest report focuses on three key areas of product success—acquisition,
engagement, and retention—going beyond surface-level trends to determine the
state of product analytics in the media and entertainment world.

Download the free report where you'll find:

- Detailed subindustry insights: We dive into the nuances of user behavior
  across key sectors—social media, publishers, streaming, gaming, creative,
  broadcasting—offering tailored insights and recommendations for each domain.
- Key metrics to track like app install rate, session duration, DAU, WAU,
  MAU, CLTV, user login sessions, churn rate, time between visits, content
  velocity, and many more.

The report also answers questions like:

- What is the month-over-month percentage user growth for different media
  sectors?
- Which global region shows the lowest mobile popularity? (Hint: It's not who
  you may think.)
- How are companies measuring and improving user engagement?
- Despite its popularity, why is engagement falling for streamers?
- Why did one week retention rates fall for all but two subindustries?
- And more…

This report doesn't just highlight the "what"—it dives deep into the "how."
Learn your subindustry's most popular metrics to track and walk away with
concrete recommendations to improve your results.

How does your company compare to the rest of the industry? Download the 2025
State of Media & Entertainment Product Analytics report today and find out.
